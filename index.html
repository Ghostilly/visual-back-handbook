01 · The lumbar spine

Five bones carry you.

The five lumbar vertebrae stack on the sacrum, the base of your spine, which sits inside the pelvis. Spin it. This is the part of you most back pain is about.

02 · The disc

A tough ring around a soft centre.

Between each pair of vertebrae sits a disc. Words like bulge, protrusion and herniation on a scan report describe the shape of this ring.

03 · Nerve roots

Small gaps, sensitive nerves.

Nerve roots leave the spine through small gaps beside each disc. When something narrows that space, a root can get irritated.

04 · Sciatica

Why your back can hurt in your leg.

Roots from the lower back join to form the sciatic nerve, which runs down the back of the leg. That's why a problem at the spine can be felt all the way down.

05 · Muscles

And it all moves together.

Deep muscles like the multifidus and psoas support and move the spine. You just explored one small corner. The handbook puts the whole map together, in order, in 3D.

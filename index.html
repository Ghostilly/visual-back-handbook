No. It's education: it explains how the back works and what the research says, so you can understand your situation and talk to your clinician. It won't diagnose you and it isn't a cure.

No. It explains what guidelines recommend and how graded activity works in principle, and how to work with a physiotherapist. Your own plan should come from a clinician who has examined you.

Yes, that's what the scan report decoder is for. It explains what the common words mean, and how often those findings show up in people with no pain at all. Your report still needs to be interpreted by your own clinician.
